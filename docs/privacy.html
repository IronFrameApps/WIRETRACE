WireTrace AI Privacy Policy

Ironframe Apps · Effective October 31, 2026

The short version

WireTrace AI has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sells or shares your data. The schematics you scan and your results are kept on your device.

Camera and photos

WireTrace AI uses the camera only when you take a picture of a wiring schematic to read. It does not take pictures on its own or access your camera at any other time.

AI reading

To read a schematic, the photo you take is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to Google's Gemini API, which returns the reading. The photo is used only to produce that reading. WireTrace AI does not keep a copy on any server. Google processes the request under the Gemini API Terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your data

Your scans, results and settings are stored only on your device.

Deleting your data

Clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Either removes everything WireTrace AI stored. There is nothing on any server to delete.

Children

WireTrace AI is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
